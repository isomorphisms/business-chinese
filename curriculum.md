# Arabic: Michigan everyday/workplace use

Goal: spoken Arabic useful in the user's actual Michigan surroundings,
especially ordinary conversations with Arab coworkers and employers.

## Drill shape

- Default sessions: about 5–10 minutes.
- Optimize for recall, comprehension, and saying useful things out loud.
- Prefer colloquial speech that is broadly usable with Yemeni, Iraqi,
  Lebanese, and other Arab coworkers over formal textbook sequencing.
- Keep dialect notes when a phrase changes materially across speakers.

## Initial domains

- greetings and names;
- where someone is from;
- how long someone has been in the United States;
- how they are liking Michigan / the United States;
- what they are having for lunch;
- whether they still talk with family back home;
- breaks, shifts, and the workday ending;
- asking what happened or what is wrong;
- offering help without sounding like a supervisor.

This branch is the broad Michigan corpus. More specialized Amazon-maintenance
and auto-shop material lives on their own branches.
