# Navajo refreshers

Goal: preserve and recover the Navajo material the user enjoyed, with short
sessions rather than a large generic course.

## Approach

- 5–10 minute vocabulary or phrase refreshers.
- Favor repeated recall and listening/speaking practice.
- Add previously encountered material as it is recovered.
- Keep pronunciation guidance attached to phrases.
- No streak, prize, league, or pressure to lengthen a session.

Build the corpus from material the user actually wants to retain instead of
filling space with a conventional beginner syllabus.
