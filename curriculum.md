# Hawaiian refreshers

Goal: preserve and recover the Hawaiian material the user enjoyed without
turning it into a long mandatory daily course.

## Approach

- 5–10 minute vocabulary or phrase refreshers.
- Repeated recall of material already encountered is useful by itself.
- Add old Duolingo vocabulary when it is recovered or re-encountered.
- Keep pronunciation and short complete phrases alongside vocabulary.
- No streak, league, prize, or escalating-session requirement.
- Stop after a useful short session without treating that as failure.

This branch can grow from actual remembered material rather than an invented
full curriculum.
