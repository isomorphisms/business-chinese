# Arabic: auto body / car shop

Goal: spoken Arabic useful if working for or alongside Arab coworkers in an
automotive or body-shop environment.

## Initial vocabulary domains

- car locations and panels: front, rear, left, right, hood, bumper, fender,
  door, quarter panel, trunk;
- visible damage: dent, scratch, crack, rust, bent, broken;
- repair state: remove, install, replace, repair, straighten, paint, dry,
  finished, waiting;
- parts and supplies: part, clip, bolt, screw, paint, primer, sandpaper;
- basic shop workflow: lift it, lower it, hold this, show me, bring this,
  where does this go, do we have another one;
- status questions: what happened, what needs to be fixed, what are we waiting
  for, is this ready.

## Working set

See [`auto-shop.md`](auto-shop.md) for the first practical automotive-shop
corpus: suspension and electrical parts, hand tools, multimeter and voltage-drop
diagnostics, movement/play/condition words, and short coworker phrases.

The existing working set is neutral Levantine/Mashreqi material. New Arabic
learning material defaults to Yemeni spoken Arabic when a genuinely Yemeni form
is known; keep other regional forms explicitly rather than silently rewriting
or relabeling them.

## Bidirectional dictionary

See [`dictionary/`](dictionary/) for the recursive Arabic–English dictionary.

Every translation written in an entry must itself have an entry in the same
directory. Reverse entries are independently translated and ordered rather
than mechanically inverted. Words, idioms, clauses, and whole phrases all use
the same representation.

The initial closed component grows from `hey old man, take a seat`, so it
immediately exercises respectful/congenial address, marked age terms, and the
`take a seat` / `sit down` phrase family.

## Coworker conversation

Include break/lunch/help language and ordinary social conversation, not only
technical nouns.

Sessions should stay short enough to be usable before or after work. Expand the
corpus from real shop interactions rather than trying to enumerate an entire
automotive dictionary first.
