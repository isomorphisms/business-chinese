# Recovered Arabic conversation variants

History pass: 2026-09-26. Default spoken learning target remains Yemeni Arabic.
This collection preserves earlier wording, including questionable wording;
it does not certify earlier assistant translations as correct or Yemeni.

The recoverable source is indexed conversation excerpts, not a complete export.
Five targeted retrievals recovered dated material from June 2025, June 2026 and
September 2026. Exhaustive coverage of every prior conversation is **not
established**. No missing Arabic script is filled in and presented as recovered.

## June 5, 2025: workplace repairs

Context: a technician offering help and conversation to Arab coworkers while
fixing their workstations. These are historical **assistant** suggestions, not
claims about phrases the user personally said. Source timestamp for this table:
2025-06-05 06:45:13 UTC.

| Meaning | Recovered Arabic | Recovered transliteration | Relation to the current seeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| What's broken? | شو الخرْبان؟ | Shū l-kharbān? | Preserve the spelling with the article; later retrieval also has شو خربان؟. Levantine form, not the Yemeni target. |
| Let me look. | خلّيني أشوف | Khalleeni ashūf | Same phrase as seed 2; transliteration spelling retained. |
| I'll fix it. | رح أصلّحو | Raḥ aṣlaḥo | Earlier spelling differs from seed رح أصلّحه. Preserve both; spelling and the older transliteration need checking. |
| Go take a break while I fix this. | روح ارتاح حتى أصلّح هادا | Rūḥ irtaḥ, ḥattā aṣlaḥ hāda | Distinct wording from the later “let me fix this” version. Keep both intentions visible. |
| Sit down, enjoy it. | اجلِس، استمتع شوية | Ijlis, istamtiʿ shwayye | Earlier mixed-register suggestion. Later seed uses اقعد، ارتاح شوي for the intended rest invitation. Retain as history, not a preferred spoken target. |
| Did you eat well? | أكلت منيح؟ | Akalt mniḥ? | Same Levantine phrase as seed 6. |
| What's your name, old man? | شو اسمك، يا شَيْب؟ | Shū ismak, yā shayb? | Omitted from the eight seeds. Regional form and social effect need review; do not teach automatically as a safe respectful address. |
| Where are you from? | من وين إنت؟ | Min wēn inta? | Preserve beside the Yemeni-target spelling/pronunciation in seed 7. |
| Come on, the day's almost over! | Not returned for this dated passage | Yalla, qarrab yikhlaṣ il-nahār! | Later seed has Arabic script; do not misattribute its script to this older passage. |
