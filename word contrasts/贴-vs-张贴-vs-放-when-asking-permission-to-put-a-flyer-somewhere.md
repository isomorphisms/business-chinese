# 贴 vs 张贴 vs 放 when asking permission to put a flyer somewhere

- **贴** means to stick or affix something to a surface and is natural in spoken "Can I put this up here?"
- **张贴** is the more formal "post/display by affixing" word used for notices, posters, and rules.
- **放** means put/place/leave and does not imply attaching the paper to anything.
- **发布** is "publish/post" in an information-distribution sense and is wrong for many physical-wall situations.

The physical action matters. A flyer taped to a wall, a stack left on a counter,
and an announcement posted online are not the same substitution problem.
