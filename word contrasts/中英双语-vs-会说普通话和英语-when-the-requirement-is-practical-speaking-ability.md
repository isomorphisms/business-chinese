# 中英双语 vs 会说普通话和英语 when the requirement is practical speaking ability

- **中英双语** labels someone as Chinese-English bilingual. It is compact, but it can suggest a fairly substantial command of both languages.
- **中英文双语** is understandable but heavier; 中英文 often foregrounds Chinese and English as language/written-language categories.
- **会说普通话和英语** directly says that the person can speak Mandarin and English.
- **会说中文和英语** keeps "Chinese" broader than Mandarin.

For a flyer whose practical test is "can this person actually help me communicate?",
the explicit ability phrase states the requirement more directly than a bilingual
identity label.
