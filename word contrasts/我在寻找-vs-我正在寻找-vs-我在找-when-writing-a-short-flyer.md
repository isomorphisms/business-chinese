# 我在寻找 vs 我正在寻找 vs 我在找 when writing a short flyer

- **我在寻找** is fairly formal and fits written copy.
- **我正在寻找** explicitly foregrounds the ongoing search and is also suitable in a flyer.
- **我在找** is shorter and more conversational.
- **我正在找** keeps the colloquial verb while emphasizing that the search is underway.

All can describe the same broad activity, but register and aspectual emphasis change.
