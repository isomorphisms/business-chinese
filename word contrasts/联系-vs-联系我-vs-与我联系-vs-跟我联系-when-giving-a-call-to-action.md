# 联系 vs 联系我 vs 与我联系 vs 跟我联系 when giving a call to action

- **请联系：** is compact flyer language when the contact details immediately follow.
- **联系我** is direct: "contact me."
- **与我联系** is more formal.
- **跟我联系** is more conversational.

The omitted object in 请联系： is recoverable from the layout. In ordinary running
speech, 联系我 or 跟我联系 usually makes the object explicit.
