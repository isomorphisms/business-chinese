# 帮助 vs 协助 vs 帮忙 when asking a stranger for paid business help

- **帮助** is the broad neutral word for helping.
- **协助** is more formal and cooperative; it fits a written request for business assistance well.
- **帮忙** is everyday and interpersonal. It often sounds like doing someone a favor, even though payment can still be stated separately.
- **帮** is short and colloquial.

The paid-flyer context can make 协助 useful because it does not sound as much like
asking for an informal favor. In ordinary spoken interaction, 帮忙 or 帮 may be more natural.
