# 您 vs 你 when addressing an unknown adult in a flyer or store

- **您** is the polite/respectful second-person form.
- **你** is the ordinary neutral second-person form.

A public flyer can use 您 for courtesy, as in 如果您有兴趣. Ordinary face-to-face
speech may use 你 without being rude. Region, age, relationship, and local habit
affect the choice, so there is no context-free ranking that holds everywhere.
