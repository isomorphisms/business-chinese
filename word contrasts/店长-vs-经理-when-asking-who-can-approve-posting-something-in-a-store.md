# 店长 vs 经理 when asking who can approve posting something in a store

Context: asking whether permission is needed to put up a flyer in a store.

- **店长** points specifically to the person in charge of the store.
- **经理** is the broader word for manager and can refer to many kinds of managerial roles.
- **门店经理** is explicit but heavier than ordinary speech.

In this physical-store situation, 店长 usually identifies the relevant role more
directly. In another workplace, 经理 may be the correct title.
