# 华人 vs 中国人 vs 会说中文的人 when the real requirement is language ability

Context: an Ann Arbor flyer asking for someone who can help with business communication.

- **华人** identifies a Chinese ethnic/cultural background. It can include people who are not Chinese nationals.
- **中国人** ordinarily identifies a person as Chinese/from China and is much closer to nationality or national identity.
- **会说中文的人** states the practical language requirement without asserting ethnicity or nationality.
- **中英双语的华人** combines two requirements: bilingual ability and Chinese identity/background.

These are not interchangeable labels for the same property. If the real criterion is
language ability, state the language ability. If identity itself matters, then an
identity term may be intentional.
