# 普通话 vs 中文 vs 汉语 vs 华语 when asking what language someone speaks

- **普通话** is specifically Standard Mandarin in mainland usage.
- **中文** is the broad everyday label for Chinese and is also used for written Chinese.
- **汉语** is a broad Chinese-language term and often sounds more linguistic or formal than 中文.
- **华语** is common in some Chinese-speaking communities outside mainland China and can be the natural regional label.

If the actual requirement is spoken Mandarin, 普通话 is the precise statement.
If the requirement is deliberately broader, 中文 may better preserve that breadth.
