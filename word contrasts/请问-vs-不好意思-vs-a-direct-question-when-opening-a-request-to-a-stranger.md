# 请问 vs 不好意思 vs a direct question when opening a request to a stranger

- **请问** politely marks that a genuine question is coming.
- **不好意思** softens an interruption or imposition and can precede either a question or a request.
- A direct question can also be polite if the wording and situation are ordinary.

Politeness should not require changing the speech act. If the speaker is actually
stating a rule or constraint, turning it into a question that appears to offer a
choice is a different communicative act, not merely a nicer version of the same sentence.
