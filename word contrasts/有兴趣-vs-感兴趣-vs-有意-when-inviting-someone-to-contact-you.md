# 有兴趣 vs 感兴趣 vs 有意 when inviting someone to contact you

- **有兴趣** is broad "have interest" and works naturally in 如果您有兴趣.
- **感兴趣** describes being interested in something and often wants the object or context to be clear.
- **有意** suggests intention or willingness, and can sound more formal or transactional.

For a flyer, 如果您有兴趣，请联系…… is a straightforward invitation. 有意 can be
useful when the intended meaning is closer to "if you intend/wish to participate."
