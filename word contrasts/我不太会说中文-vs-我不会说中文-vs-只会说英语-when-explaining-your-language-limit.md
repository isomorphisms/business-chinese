# 我不太会说中文 vs 我不会说中文 vs 只会说英语 when explaining your language limit

- **我不太会说中文** says "I am not very able to speak Chinese" and leaves room for some ability.
- **我不怎么会说中文** is similar and conversational.
- **我不会说中文** is stronger: "I cannot/do not know how to speak Chinese."
- **我只会说英语** says "I only speak English" and makes the positive restriction explicit.
- **我的中文不太好** says "my Chinese is not very good," which implies some Chinese.

Choose according to the actual claim about ability. These are not merely more and
less polite versions of one proposition.
