# 商务沟通 vs 商务交流 vs 业务沟通 when describing the kind of help needed

- **商务沟通** is a broad expression for business communication and matches the current flyer intention well.
- **商务交流** leans toward exchange, interaction, networking, or mutual sharing in a business setting.
- **业务沟通** often points toward communication about concrete operations, work, accounts, or business matters.
- **商业沟通** is understandable but can sound more like a category label than the ordinary task in this flyer.

The intended work should determine the phrase. "Business communication" in English
does not force one Chinese noun phrase in every setting.
