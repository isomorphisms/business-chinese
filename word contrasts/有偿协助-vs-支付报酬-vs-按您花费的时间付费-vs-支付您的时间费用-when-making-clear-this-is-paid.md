# 有偿协助 vs 支付报酬 vs 按您花费的时间付费 vs 支付您的时间费用 when making clear this is paid

- **这是有偿协助** classifies the arrangement compactly as paid assistance.
- **我会支付报酬** explicitly promises compensation but does not say how it is calculated.
- **我会按您花费的时间付费** says payment is based on time spent. It is more concrete, but it commits to a time-based basis.
- **我愿意支付您的时间费用** is understandable, but it follows English "pay for your time" rather literally and is stiffer than the alternatives above.

These differ in commitment, not just style. Do not use the time-based wording merely
as a synonym if the intended deal has not actually been specified that way.
