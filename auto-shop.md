# Spanish: automotive shop working set

Goal: fill practical shop gaps for an already-functional Spanish speaker. Prefer
Guatemalan/Central American usage where a regional choice matters, and use voseo
in coworker phrases.

## First drills

Use short shop exchanges, not beginner vocabulary quizzes:

1. `Mirá esto.` → answer `está flojo`, `está oxidado`, `está duro`, or `está firme`.
2. `¿Tiene juego?` → answer `tiene juego` or `no tiene juego`.
3. `Medí el voltaje aquí.` → give a reading, then `revisá la tierra`.
4. `Movelo mientras medís.` → say whether the reading stays stable or changes.
5. `Busquemos la falla.` → choose cable, conector, fusible, relé, tierra, or sensor as the next check.
